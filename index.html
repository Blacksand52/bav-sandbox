How is my security protected?
This demo never touches a real bank account. If BAV became real: no bank logins or passwords would ever be stored by BAV. Access would go through UK Open Banking — the same regulated, read-only, revocable standard already used by Monzo, Starling, and other UK banks — never screen-scraping or password storage. You could revoke access at any time from your own bank's app.
